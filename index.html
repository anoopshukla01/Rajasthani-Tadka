Stage 01 • Thar Desert

The Whispering Sands of Marwar

Under the amber glow of the desert sunset, our journey begins where wild Ker berries and Sangri beans thrive amidst undulating dunes, shaped by ancient winds and untamed warrior spirit.

Stage 02 • Village Hearth

Woodfire Embers & Stone Mills

In rural Shekhawati hamlets, the morning rhythm echoes with hand-beaten bajra rotis puffing over clay chulhas, golden cow ghee melting over earthen pots, and hand-pounded Mathania spices filling the crisp air.

Stage 03 • Royal Court

The Grand Haveli Threshold

Carved sandstone arches welcome the weary traveller into fragrant courtyards of marigold and rosewater. Here, royal Khansamas historically crafted feasts fit for kings with recipes guarded as royal state secrets.

Stage 04 • Arrival

"From the heart of Rajasthan, served with a modern touch." We invite you to step through our threshold and partake in the royal culinary ritual of Mewar and Marwar.
